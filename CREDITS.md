# Credits and asset provenance

## DEQANT artwork

The official DEQANT PDF and EPS artwork was supplied by the user for this project. Original source files are retained in `brand-assets/` as `deqant-official-logo.pdf` and `deqant-official-logo.eps`.

The transparent logos, emblems, pattern, and favicon in `public/brand/` are format conversions of that supplied artwork. They are not replacement logos. The eight compositions in `public/products/` were created for this storefront and embed the converted official wordmark; they are original presentation illustrations, not photographs of physical inventory.

All DEQANT trademarks and supplied brand artwork remain the property of their respective owner.
